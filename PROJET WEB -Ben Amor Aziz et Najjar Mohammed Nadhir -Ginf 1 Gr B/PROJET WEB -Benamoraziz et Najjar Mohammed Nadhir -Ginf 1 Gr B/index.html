How To Play

  • Move with Arrow keys, WASD, or ZQSD.
  • Reach any glowing exit on the right wall.
  • Chain chest pickups quickly to build combo streak bonus.
  • Use abilities during a run: H Hint Path (cost 1 coin), F Freeze Enemies (cost 2 coins).
  • Enemy hits consume lives instead of immediate game over.

Space = Start/Pause | R = New Maze | Esc = Close results
